AstroCode is an interactive tool designed to make CAPE / A-level Physics more digestible. Choose a projectile-motion situation, select a planet, and adjust the launch conditions to see the path of the object unfold. It connects your values to the results and the working behind them, so you can investigate how speed, angle, height, and gravity change a projectile’s motion.
